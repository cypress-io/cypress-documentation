The charts don't rely on color. Each series has its own marker shape and line
style, each color reaches at least 3:1 contrast against the chart background,
and the legend sits above the plot. Under the charts, a **Latest runs** table
lists the 20 most recent runs on the trend branch as text, including each run's
Run Checks result and the checks it failed. To describe a chart to screen
reader users, open its **⋮** menu, choose **Alt text**, and summarize what it
shows.
